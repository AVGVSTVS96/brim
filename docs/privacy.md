# Privacy

Span collects nothing.

- **No data leaves your iPhone.** Neither the app nor the Safari extension makes network requests.
- **Nothing is stored.** Span keeps no history, settings, accounts or identifiers.
- **No tracking, analytics or ads.**

Safari asks you to let Span read and change the web pages you visit, because laying a page out full width means
measuring it and changing its layout. It looks only at where things sit on the page and how wide they are, all within
Safari on your iPhone, and nothing it sees is kept or sent anywhere.

The code is open: [github.com/AVGVSTVS96/brim](https://github.com/AVGVSTVS96/brim).
